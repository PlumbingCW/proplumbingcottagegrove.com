What to tell the dispatcher

Have these ready

  • Your address and a callback number
  • What is happening: leak, backup, no hot water or something else
  • Whether water is spreading and whether you have shut off the main
  • Any water near outlets or the electrical panel
  • Pets or people who may be affected

The more you tell us, the better the plumber is prepared.
