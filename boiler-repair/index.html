Boiler emergency: no heat, when to call right away

Gas smell or carbon monoxide alarm

Leave the home and call 911 or the gas utility. Do not use switches or phones inside. A plumber can inspect the boiler after the area is declared safe.

A boiler heats water and sends it through radiators, baseboard or in-floor loops. When it fails in sub-zero weather, pipes can freeze in unheated spaces within hours, and a split pipe adds a flood to the cold.

Call at once if the boiler is leaking, if the pressure gauge is far out of range, if the burner will not light or if the unit is making banging or kettle noises.

Boilers in east metro homes

Many older Cottage Grove homes, especially those built before the 1970s growth in the city, use hot water boilers with radiators or baseboard. The system is quiet and comfortable, but it has more moving parts than people expect, including circulator pumps, zone valves, an expansion tank and a pressure relief valve.

A boiler that is more than twenty years old may still run well with maintenance, but parts get harder to find and efficiency falls. Ask whether the boiler is a standard or a condensing model, since condensing boilers need a drain for condensate and a plastic vent.

What a service visit covers

Boiler service checklist

  • Check the water pressure and the expansion tank
  • Inspect the burner and the heat exchanger
  • Test the circulator and zone valves
  • Check the relief valve and the vent
  • Test the safety controls and low-water cutoff
  • Look for leaks at fittings and the cold fill

Annual service reduces the chance of a midwinter failure.

Repair

  • A failed pump or valve
  • A burner or ignition fault
  • A leaking fitting
  • Boiler under about fifteen years

Replace

  • Cracked heat exchanger
  • Frequent repairs
  • Old, inefficient unit
  • Corrosion through the shell

Protecting pipes when the boiler is down

  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls
  • Let a faucet drip on pipes at risk
  • Use space heaters safely and never leave them unattended
  • Know where the main water shutoff is
  • Call the plumber early, since a frozen pipe can burst at thaw

If a pipe has already split, see burst pipe repair. If hot water comes from the boiler, a failed unit can also affect your water heater.

Radiators, baseboard and in-floor heat

Each type of boiler heat behaves differently when something goes wrong. Cast iron radiators hold heat for a long time and can trap air at the top. Baseboard heat reacts quickly and often has one zone valve per room group. In-floor loops are quiet and even, but a leak in the slab is much harder to reach, which is where leak detection helps.

If only one room is cold, the issue is local, such as a stuck zone valve or air in a single loop. If the whole house is cold, look at the boiler, the circulator and the thermostat first. Knowing which applies helps us bring the right parts.

Keep the area around the boiler clear for service and combustion air, and never store paint, solvents or cleaners close to it.

Code, permits and standards

Boilers have their own safety standards for pressure relief, low-water cutoffs and venting, set out in the manufacturer's guidelines and in state and local rules. Plumbing connections follow the Minnesota Plumbing Code, so ask the City of Cottage Grove which permits apply to a replacement.
