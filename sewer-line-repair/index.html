Sewer backup: what to do first

  1. Stop using water, including toilets, sinks, showers and the washing machine
  2. Keep children and pets away from the affected area
  3. Switch off power only if water is near outlets and you can do so from a dry spot
  4. Open windows if the odor is strong and call the 24/7 line
  5. Document the damage with photos before cleanup, in case you file a claim

Our dispatcher will ask which drains are involved so a plumber arrives knowing where to start. When wastewater is already on the floor, treat it as contaminated and avoid skin contact.

Repair options and honest trade-offs

Open-cut repair

  • Fixes a specific broken section quickly
  • Lets us regrade a belly properly
  • Requires digging up a lawn, driveway or slab
  • Works when the pipe has collapsed

Trenchless lining

  • Needs far less digging
  • Seals cracks and root entry points
  • Pipe must be clear and round enough to line
  • Not a fit for a major belly or collapse

We explain which of those fits your pipe and why, and we confirm the price before work starts. If a spot repair solves the problem, we will not sell a full replacement.

Where the repair crosses the street or touches the city main, the work involves the city, so a plumber will tell you what needs a permit and who to call.

Older sewer lines in Cottage Grove

Much of Cottage Grove was built out in the 1970s and 1990s, and some of the oldest parts of the city began as farms and township lots, with historic properties such as Cedarhurst Mansion near the southeastern end. Homes of different eras carry different pipe, from clay and cast iron on the oldest lots to PVC and ABS on newer ones.

That mix matters when a line fails. A clay line near large trees is a candidate for root damage. A newer PVC line is more likely to have a bellied section from settling backfill after construction.

Questions to ask before approving any sewer repair

  • Has a camera shown the actual defect and where it is?
  • Is the break on my side of the property line or the city side?
  • Will the work need a permit and who pulls it?
  • What does the quote include for restoring the yard or driveway?
  • Is there an option that fixes only the damaged section?

You should see the video or hear a plain description of what the camera shows before agreeing to dig.

Before you dig, call 811

Any excavation near utilities requires a locate. In Minnesota the one-call line is 811 through Gopher State One Call. A plumber will arrange locates for the work site, and you should never dig on your own without one.

What to expect on repair day

Before digging, the route is located and marked through 811, and we confirm where the pipe enters the house and where it meets the city connection. Water to the house stays on in most cases, though sewer use is limited while the line is open.

After the repair, the line is tested and, when it is open to view, a camera confirms the new section is clear and sloped properly. You also get a clear description of what was found, which helps if you later sell the home or make an insurance claim.

Code, permits and standards

Sewer repair is regulated work. State rules require installations to meet the Minnesota Plumbing Code, and inspections happen where a city acts as the administrative authority. Ask the City of Cottage Grove which permit and inspection steps apply to a lateral repair at your address.
