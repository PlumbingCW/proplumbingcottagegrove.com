Cottage Grove neighborhoods and landmarks

Within Cottage Grove, we work across the city, from the Kingston Park area and the Highland Park area to the neighborhoods near Oak Wood Park and Ravine Regional Park. The Grey Cloud Island area, on the Mississippi riverfront, has older homes and lower ground, and river-side lots often have damp basements and sump needs.

Major roads help with planning. U.S. 61 and East Point Douglas Road South carry most traffic, and Interstate 494 connects the city to the rest of the metro. For businesses and schools, the corridor includes East Ridge High School and Park High School, and the long-running 3M plant, which is one of the largest employers.

Saint Paul Park and Newport

Saint Paul Park (55071) and Newport (55055) sit between Cottage Grove and Saint Paul along the river. Homes there are older than in most of Cottage Grove, so we see more cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines and sewer lateral roots. Basements near the river can take on water during spring melt.

If you are in those towns, the pages most useful to you are drain cleaning, sewer line repair and sump pump service.

Inver Grove Heights

Inver Grove Heights, with ZIP codes 55076 and 55077, is a larger suburb west of Cottage Grove across the Dakota County line. Its housing mix includes mid-century homes, newer subdivisions and commercial corridors. Calls there often involve water heaters, drains and leaks.

Check with a dispatcher on timing, since distance and traffic vary. For emergencies, the 24/7 line works the same as it does in Cottage Grove.

Water and utilities by town

Each town has its own water utility and its own permit office. Hastings Public Works publishes the city's annual drinking water report and PFAS updates. Cottage Grove residents can ask City of Cottage Grove Public Works for the same kind of report.

For plumbing permits and inspections, contact the building department in your city. According to the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry, plumbing installations must meet the Minnesota Plumbing Code and that required inspections must be performed.
