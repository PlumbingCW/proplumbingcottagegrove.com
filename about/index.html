How we work

When you call, a dispatcher takes the details, tells you what to do while you wait and schedules a licensed plumber, the same day when available. The plumber looks at the problem, explains what is wrong and confirms a clear price before work starts.

We aim to be plain about what we find. If a repair will fix the problem, we say so. If a replacement is the better call, we explain why and show you the evidence.

What you can count on

  • A line answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays
  • Licensed and insured plumbers
  • A clear price before work starts
  • Same-day appointments when available
  • Work that follows the Minnesota Plumbing Code

These are the commitments we make. We do not publish made-up ratings, review counts or awards. If we cannot back a claim with something real, we leave it off the site.

Where we work

We are based at 8711 East Point Douglas Road South in Cottage Grove. The city lies on the north bank of the Mississippi River in Washington County, and its homes range from older township lots to subdivisions built during the growth of the 1970s through the 2000s.

That mix shapes our work. Older lines mean root intrusion and cast iron, newer homes mean water heater and fixture service, and river-side lots mean sump pumps and basement moisture. We also serve Hastings, Saint Paul Park, Newport and Inver Grove Heights. See the service area for details.

Licensing and standards

Plumbing in Minnesota is regulated. The Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry states that plumbing installations must meet the Minnesota Plumbing Code and that required inspections must be performed. Cities act as the administrative authority in many places, and we follow the permit steps that apply to your address.

We do not list license numbers on this site. If you want to confirm a plumber's license, you can check with the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry and ask us for details when you call.

What we do

Our services include drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection, sewer line repair, burst pipe repair and commercial plumbing. The full list is on the services page.

For emergencies, the first step is the same every time. Shut off the water if you can, stay clear of electrical hazards and call the 24/7 line.

What we will not do

We will not push a replacement when a repair will hold. We will not promise an arrival time that traffic and weather can break. We will not list reviews, awards or years in business that we cannot show you.

If a job is outside what we do, such as exterior waterproofing or foundation repair, we say so and suggest you find the right trade. A short honest answer on the phone is better than a wasted visit.
