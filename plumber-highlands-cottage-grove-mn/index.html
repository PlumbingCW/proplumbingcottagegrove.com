Where Highlands is

Highlands Park is the best-known landmark in the area, and a splash pad opened there in 2012. People use the name Highlands for the neighborhood around the park, but it is informal, and the edges are not fixed.

We work from your address, not a map line. Give the dispatcher your street and we confirm whether it falls in the area we serve.

What calls look like in a residential area like this

Homes in a suburban neighborhood tend to ask more of the water heater, the kitchen and the bathrooms than the main line. A family home with several bathrooms uses hot water at the same time, so one failing tank is felt right away.

We do not assume the age of your plumbing from the address. We read the label on the heater, check the supply lines and tell you what we find before recommending anything.

Water heater trouble

Cold showers, a rumbling tank or water pooling near the base are the usual signs. See water heater repair and water heater replacement.

Tankless options

If you want endless hot water, ask about a tankless water heater. We explain venting, gas and electrical needs before you decide.

Drain and disposal problems

A kitchen sink that backs up or a disposal that hums but does not turn needs service. See drain cleaning and garbage disposal service.

Fixture leaks

A dripping faucet or running toilet wastes water. See faucet repair and toilet repair.
