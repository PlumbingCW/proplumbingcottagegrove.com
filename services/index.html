Drains and sewers

Drain Cleaning

A drain that has stopped moving water is an emergency the moment it backs up into a tub, a floor drain or a second fixture.

Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting is what a licensed plumber turns to when a drain keeps backing up after cabling.

Sewer Line Repair

A failed sewer line can put wastewater into a basement within hours.

Sewer Line Cleaning

When the main sewer line clogs, every drain in the house is affected, and the lowest one overflows first.

Sewer Camera Inspection

A sewer camera inspection shows what is happening inside the pipe without digging.

Water heaters

Water Heater Repair

A water heater that leaks, floods or goes cold in a Minnesota winter needs attention the same day.

Water Heater Replacement

When a tank water heater is leaking from its body, replacement is the only fix, and it cannot wait.

Water Heater Installation

A planned water heater installation lets you choose the unit, the location and the schedule instead of reacting to a failure.

Tankless Water Heater

A tankless water heater heats water only when you open a tap, so it can supply hot water for as long as the burner or element runs.

Leaks and pipes

Leak Detection

A hidden leak does damage quietly until a stain, a smell or a water bill gives it away.

Burst Pipe Repair

A burst pipe can put a hundred gallons into your home in minutes.

Water Service Line Repair

The water service line carries water from the main in the street to your house, and when it breaks, you can lose pressure, flood a yard or see a sudden spike on your bill.

Repiping

Repiping replaces the supply pipes that run through your walls, floors and ceilings.

Fixtures

Toilet Repair and Installation

A toilet that will not stop running, is overflowing or leaks at the base can flood a bathroom quickly.

Faucet and Sink Repair

A faucet that will not shut off or a sink supply that is spraying is a water emergency.

Garbage Disposal Repair

A jammed or leaking garbage disposal can block the kitchen sink and flood the cabinet below.

Plumbing Installation

New plumbing work should be planned before walls are closed, because the pipes behind them decide how well everything works.

Basements and seasonal

Sump Pump Service

A sump pump that quits during a heavy rain or a spring thaw can put water on your basement floor in minutes.

Basement Waterproofing

Basement seepage has a cause, and the fix starts with finding it.

Boiler Repair

A boiler that fails in January is an emergency for the pipes as well as the people.

Water Softener and Filtration

Mineral scale, spotted glasses and stiff laundry are signs of water that carries dissolved minerals, but not every home needs a softener.

Commercial Plumbing

When plumbing fails in a business, the cost is measured in lost hours as well as repairs.
